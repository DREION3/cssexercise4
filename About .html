Meet the Founder


Built by an outsider, for the outsiders. Built by an outsider, for the outsiders. NOREID isn't a faceless corporation backed by corporate investors; it is a passion proj ect brought to life by someone who has lived and breathed alternative subcultures from day one. As the founder, my journey into the alternative scene wasn’t just a phase—it was a way of finding my footing in a world that often demands conformity. Frustrated by the lack of genuinely authentic, high-quality gear that spoke our language without watering it down, I decided to stop waiting for someone else to build it. When you shop with NOREID, you aren’t just supporting a business—you’re backing someone who is right there in the trenches with you, fighting against the beige, cookie-cutter aesthetic of the mainstream world.
